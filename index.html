Firefox

Add-on · version 1.4.0

Capture Studio

Take the screenshot, mark it up, and put it somewhere useful — without leaving the tab. Recording, video download and transcripts are in the same add-on, on the same seven keys.

  • Firefox 140+
  • Manifest V3
  • No account required

Keys

Seven commands, and all of them are a chord

Ctrl+Alt+V
Visible part
What is on screen in the active tab, as it is.
Ctrl+Alt+C
After a countdown
Three, five or ten seconds, counted on the page — long enough to open the menu you wanted in the shot.
Ctrl+Alt+A
Selected area
Drag a rectangle. Cropped at the display’s real pixel ratio, so a retina screen gives a retina crop.
Ctrl+Alt+F
Full page
Scrolls and stitches the whole scrollable page into one tall image.
Ctrl+Alt+E
Annotate
Opens the editor on a file, on the clipboard, or on a blank surface.
Ctrl+Alt+D
Download this video
Asks the site what it actually has, then offers it.
Ctrl+Alt+T
Transcribe this video
Turns the captions into a note whose timestamps jump back into the video.

Every one of them is rebindable in Firefox’s own shortcut settings, and every one has a menu item too.

Annotate

An editor that keeps annotations as annotations

Pen, line, arrow, box, text, highlighter and eraser, each on a letter key. Everything you draw stays an object: select it later and move it, turn it, restyle it, or take it off again. The picture underneath is never flattened until you export.

  • Pixelate samples the picture as it stands and redraws it in blocks, so the address or the customer’s name is gone from the image rather than covered by something sitting on top of it.
  • Crop & split cuts one screenshot into a grid of separate PNGs, dragging the lines rather than typing numbers.
  • Out as a PNG download, onto the clipboard, into Google Drive, or as a share link.

Record

Screen or camera, straight to a file

The desktop, a window, a tab, or the webcam — with system audio and the microphone as separate switches. It comes out as a .webm you can play anywhere, and the recording never goes through a server: the browser writes the file and hands it to you.

Video

Download, and what was said in it

A Download button appears on the YouTube page in the row with Like and Share, because that is where you look for something to do with the video you are watching. It offers the resolutions the video really carries — not a generic list — plus audio only as MP3, M4A or Opus, each with an estimated size. A playlist becomes one download per video, all running at once.

The transcript is the video’s own captions, laid out as paragraphs or as a two-column table you can annotate in place. Every timestamp is a link back into the video at that second.

Drive

Optional, and it is your own Drive

Sign in and captures, recordings and notes can go to Google Drive, with several accounts signed in at once and a chip on the editor toolbar naming the folder the next upload lands in. Sharing sets the two states Drive actually has — private, or anyone with the link — and hands you the link.

There is a notes window on top of it: browse the tree, write Markdown, run a Python block in the page, open a .drawio diagram. None of it is required, and none of it runs unless you sign in.

What it does not do

  • No speech recognition. A transcript is the captions the video already has. A video without them has no transcript, and the panel says so rather than offering to invent one.
  • No server of its own. Screenshots, recordings and annotations are handled in the browser. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you upload it to your own Drive.
  • The downloader needs a helper. Video download runs through a small Python program installed separately on your machine. Everything else works without it.
  • One remote frame. Editing a diagram opens draw.io’s own editor in a pane, which loads from diagrams.net. The pane says so, and there is a route that does not use it.

Install

From the packaged add-on

  1. Build the package: powershell -File build-xpi.ps1
  2. Open about:debugging → This Firefox → Load Temporary Add-on, and pick manifest.json from the source folder — or install the signed .zip from dist/.
  3. For video download, install the native helper under native/. The add-on runs fine without it; the download panel will say the helper is missing.
